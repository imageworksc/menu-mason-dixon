The menu, on the house design system

Every colour, type size, radius, shadow and easing curve below comes from DESIGN.md in the main site repository. Nothing here invents a new value.

Tier 1 — Utility

The 24/7 status line on --color-navy-dark, matching the site's alert bar, plus Careers and the Patient Intake Form. Below 1120px the quick links move into the drawer and the status centres itself.

Tier 2 — Main

Logo, five dropdowns and the phone number. Referrals live under Contact Us; there is no separate "For Veterinarians" tab. The bar scales down in three stages between 1300px and 1120px before it becomes a drawer, so the labels never reach the wordmark.

Keyboard & pointer

Panels open on hover with a 300ms grace period on the way out, and on Enter or ↓ from the keyboard. Arrows, Home and End move within a panel; Esc closes the panel first, then the drawer. The open drawer traps focus and hands it back to the toggle on close.

Still open

Two destinations — Is This an Emergency? and FAQs — are new pages that do not exist on the live site yet. Internal links are absolute paths meant for the production domain, so they do not resolve on this preview.
