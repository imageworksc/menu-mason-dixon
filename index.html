Mason Dixon Animal Emergency Hospital

Navigation

A two-tier header built on the house design system. Every value comes from DESIGN.md, and the service copy is the site's own.

Structure
A utility tier carrying the 24/7 status, Careers and the intake form, then the main bar: wordmark, five dropdowns, phone number. Referrals sit under Contact Us rather than in a separate For Veterinarians tab.
Panels
Thirty destinations in ten named groups. Emergency Care and Our Hospital open across two columns; Symptoms leads with Is This an Emergency?; Contact closes on the address and the number.
Each panel is sized to its longest title, so nothing wraps to a second line.
Type
Be Vietnam Pro 600–800 for labels and titles, Inter 400–600 for everything else. Panel titles sit at 0.875rem, group labels at 0.6875rem.
Colour
Navy #242667, red #c01d2e, slate #56566a. The red is spent on one thing per panel at most, so it still means something when it appears.
Widths
The bar steps down at 1300, 1240 and 1120, where it becomes a drawer, then again at 600 and 380. Checked at 43 widths from 320 to 2560: no overflow, no collisions, every panel contained on a 720p screen.
Keyboard
↓ opens a panel and enters it; arrows, Home and End move inside; Esc closes the panel first and the drawer second. An open drawer traps focus and returns it to the toggle.
Not yet live
Is This an Emergency? and FAQs are new pages. Internal links are absolute paths meant for the production domain, so they do not resolve here.
